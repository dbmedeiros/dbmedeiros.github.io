Publications Curriculum Vitae

My research examines political institutions, legislative politics, coalition presidentialism, constitutional change, and elite behavior in Brazil and Latin America. I combine quantitative methods, text-as-data approaches, and comparative institutional analysis to understand how political actors structure competition and policy-making.
