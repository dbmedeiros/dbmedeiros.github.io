Danilo B. Medeiros

Assistant Professor of Political Science
Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG), Brazil

I study political institutions, legislative politics, coalition presidentialism, and constitutional change in Brazil and Latin America. My work combines quantitative methods, text-as-data approaches, and comparative institutional analysis to explain how political actors structure competition and policy-making.
